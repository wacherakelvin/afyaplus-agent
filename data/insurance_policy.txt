AfyaPlus Insurance Policy Guidelines - 2026

1. COVERAGE LIMITS:
Standard Tier covers up to $5,000 annually for outpatient procedures.
Premium Tier covers up to $20,000 annually for both outpatient and inpatient.

2. MEDICATION REIMBURSEMENT:
Amoxicillin is fully covered under both tiers.
Paracetamol is covered up to $50 annually.
Specialty drugs require a pre-authorization form signed by the attending physician.

3. EMERGENCY ROUTING:
All patients reporting severe chest pain or difficulty breathing must be routed to the Emergency Room immediately, regardless of insurance status.
