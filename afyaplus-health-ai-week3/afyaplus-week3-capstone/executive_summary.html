Memorandum: AfyaPlus Generative AI Layer, Quality, Cost and Risk

To: CTO; Medical Director   From: Olu, AI Engineering   Date: 12 November 2026

Simulation notice: model answers and clinical scores were produced in mock mode (no live API calls). Re-run with API credit before acting on these figures.

Executive summary

We tested gpt-4o and gpt-4o-mini on 15 clinical questions across USSD, Mobile App and Web Portal. On a 1–5 clinical review scale gpt-4o averaged 4.9 and gpt-4o-mini 4.2; 4 of 6 model–channel combinations met every safety threshold. Sending each feature to the cheapest model that meets those thresholds moves monthly running cost (API fees plus clinician review) from $37,404.08 to $1,579.20, and drift monitoring first flagged a shift in patient message length in Month 2.

Quality performance

An independent AI reviewer (gpt-4o acting as judge) scored each answer against an approved clinical protocol. Correctness = clinically accurate; groundedness = makes no claims beyond the protocol, which is our guard against made-up advice ("hallucination"). Safety threshold: average ≥ 4/5 and no single answer below 3/5.

  • gpt-4o: correctness 4.6/5 (±0.5 between answers), groundedness 5.0/5, relevance 5.0/5, helpfulness 4.9/5; weakest single answer scored 4/5 for correctness; wording overlap with the protocol (ROUGE-L, 0–1) 0.92. Meets safety thresholds for: Web Portal health FAQ, Mobile App medication help, USSD triage.
  • gpt-4o-mini: correctness 3.9/5 (±0.9 between answers), groundedness 4.3/5, relevance 4.6/5, helpfulness 4.0/5; weakest single answer scored 3/5 for correctness; wording overlap with the protocol (ROUGE-L, 0–1) 0.74. Meets safety thresholds for: Web Portal health FAQ.

Cost & efficiency

  • 30-day API spend at today's split (75% gpt-4o-mini, 25% gpt-4o): $152.40 for 152,870 requests. gpt-4o handles 25% of requests but accounts for 85% of spend.
  • Cost per request: gpt-4o-mini $0.00020, gpt-4o $0.00339 (17× more).
  • Clinician review is the bigger cost: answers from a model that failed a channel's safety check need 100% review at $0.40 each (assumed ~2 minutes of clinical officer time), adding $37,251.68 per month, 244× the API bill.
  • Routing by safety check: API fees $152.40 → $356.24; clinician review $37,251.68 → $1,222.96; net saving $35,824.88 (96%).

Operational risks

  • Drift (a statistically significant change in incoming patient messages or answer quality compared with launch, measured on three months of simulated traffic): Month 2: answer quality (ROUGE-L) (-3% vs launch); Month 2: patient message length (+41% vs launch); Month 3: answer quality (ROUGE-L) (-18% vs launch); Month 3: response time (+32% vs launch); Month 3: patient message length (+66% vs launch).
  • Hallucination: 0 of 15 gpt-4o-mini answers and 0 of 15 gpt-4o answers scored 2/5 or lower on groundedness; 0 reviewer outputs could not be read.
  • Budget: day-30 spend is 98% of the $6.00 daily cap; on current growth, next month is projected at $169.95 (106% of the $160.00 monthly cap).

Recommended actions

  1. Route each channel by safety check: Web Portal health FAQ → gpt-4o-mini; Mobile App medication help → gpt-4o; USSD triage → gpt-4o. Saves $35,824.88 per month, and every live model meets the clinical thresholds.
  2. Shorten the system prompt: Moving from the chain of thought prompt to zero shot cuts API fees by $205.55 per month (58%). Safety condition: roll out only after the shorter prompt passes the same 15-question safety check.
  3. Re-test automatically when drift is detected: when the alert log flags a change, automatically re-run the 15-question safety check (API cost $0.02 per run) and freeze routing changes until it passes, so declining quality is caught before it reaches patients.
